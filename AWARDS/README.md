# CSCTF 2026 awards — Team Dente

Two submissions for the CSCTF 2026 team awards:

- **[Best AI-Assisted Methodology](Best-AI-Assisted-Methodology/README.md)** — a case study of AI used as a fallible instrument, with four concrete episodes where a model output was wrong and the artifact that falsified it. Covers 9159, 9164, 9165, and 9125, and ends with the rules we now enforce.
- **[Best Technical Writeup — Phantom Device (9164)](Best-Technical-Writeup/README.md)** — the full AFC heap-overflow → two-hop tcache poisoning → House-of-Apple 2 FSOP chain, including the single-receive ceiling, the `_IO_2_1_stdin_` payload, the fd-0 trigger, and the live flag.

Both documents cite the team's working-note archive under `wave3/` and use only measurements recorded there.
